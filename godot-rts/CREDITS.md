# Credits

Tiny Realms' game code, procedural art (buildings, terrain, trees, mines, effects, UI)
and the shaders are original to this project.

The **unit sprites** and **animal sprites** are third-party pixel art used under open
licences. They are attributed below as those licences require. Modified versions
(composited, recoloured and downscaled by `tools/build_unit_sprites.py` and
`tools/build_animal_sprites.py`) are shared under the same licences.

## Animals

- **Sheep, wild boar** (`art/animals/sheep.png`, `boar.png`): "LPC style farm animals"
